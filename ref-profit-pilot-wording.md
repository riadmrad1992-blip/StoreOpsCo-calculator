# Helper text used in Profit Pilot (reuse so both products read the same)

- **Percentages:** type plain numbers without the % sign: for 9.5%, type 9.5.
- **Fee %:** the Etsy transaction fee plus payment processing, as one number (US default 9.5).
- **Fixed fee:** the flat fees on every order: the $0.20 listing fee plus the $0.25 processing fee (default 0.45).
- **Ads buffer:** a small set-aside for Etsy's Offsite Ads. If about 1 in 5 of your sales come from offsite ads, enter 3.
- **Refund allowance:** the share of sales you expect to refund (default 2).
- **Target margin:** the share of the sale price you want left as profit. For a 30% margin, type 30.
- **Typical discount:** if you often run 10% off, enter 10.
- **Shipping charged to buyer:** what the buyer pays you for shipping on one order.
- **Etsy Ads per sale:** what you spend on Etsy Ads divided by your sales. $30 in ads and 60 sales = $0.50. Leave it empty if you don't run Etsy Ads.
- **Marketing per sale:** any marketing you pay for outside Etsy for this product: social media ads, an influencer, a giveaway. Update it once a month: divide this month's marketing spend for the product by the number you sold this month. $30 on TikTok ads and 12 sales = $2.50. Leave it empty if you didn't market the product this month.
- **Material cost per unit:** pack price ÷ pack quantity × the amount one product uses. $35 ÷ 4,500 g × 70 g = $0.54.
- **Your time:** minutes per product × hourly rate ÷ 60. 10 min × $20/hr = $3.33. "Your time is a cost. Even $10/hr beats $0."
